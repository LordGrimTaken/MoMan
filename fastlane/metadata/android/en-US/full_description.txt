MoMan is a calm, fast reader for manhua, manga and webtoons, built for long-strip reading.

It ships with no content of its own: you add third-party extensions for the sources you use, and everything you read, download and track stays on your device.

Highlights
• Gapless long-strip reader — vertical continuous scrolling by default
• A library you own: categories, read state, downloads and backups, all local
• Extension sources, installed by you — the app itself ships with none
• Tracking with AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Shikimori, Bangumi, MangaBaka, MangaUpdates, Hikka, Comick, Kavita, Komga and Suwayomi
• Per-chapter downloads with a queue and automatic cleanup
• Paged and long-strip modes, dual-page spreads, colour filters, brightness and orientation controls
• Ink & Seal theme, Material You, and a dozen palettes
• No ads, no analytics, no telemetry and no account

MoMan is free and open source (MIT for its own work, Apache-2.0 for the code inherited from Mihon). It is not affiliated with any content provider and does not host or distribute content.
